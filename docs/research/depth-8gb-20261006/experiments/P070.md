## Question

Without the pool race, is min-Q (patch 0043) neutral, better or worse on Turing for lookup decode and prefill?

## Why

Notebook: "P61 had 2 of 22 lookup items with 1 token (pool on), so its 30k means compare different item sets."

## Plan and gate

Notebook: "Rerun with the PR #10 launcher (pool off, lookup on, F16 32768): 8a6d2ea12 vs 2096db283, lookup_ab.py at
0 / 16000 / 30000 and fresh 30k / 60k quick_tps, alternated. Reading: data for #330 (posted by the 4070 session in one
combined comment)." No gate.

## Method

Per engine (tier, then minq): one server with lookup_ab.py at 0, 16000, 30000 (extending); then one fresh server each
for quick_tps --depth 30000 and --depth 60000. The script runs tier then minq (not alternated within a depth).

## Results

lookup_ab means per kind (tok/s), notebook values (tier vs minq):

| Kind | d0 | d16k | d30k |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| quote | 124.5 vs 125.5 | 110.4 vs 109.8 | 103.1 vs 99.8 |
| edit | 58.5 vs 60.1 | 72.5 vs 70.2 | 66.6 vs 65.3 |
| plain | 52.1 vs 52.7 | 45.3 vs 45.0 | 53.6 vs 53.0 |

Fresh prefill (code prompt):

| Engine | 30k prefill (29,984 tok) | 30k mean decode | 60k prefill (59,953 tok) | 60k mean decode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tier | 339.4 | 42.7 | 253.5 | 29.1 |
| minq | 343.0 | 43.3 | 253.3 | 29.1 |

Texts: every item has the same sha in both engines at each depth (33 / 33). One-token: 0 of 66 lookup items,
0 quick_tps.
Receipt detail: the d30k "plain prose" item decodes at 70.1 / 68.7 tok/s (400 tokens) because it ended with
lookup-friendly text; this raises the d30k plain mean.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- Not alternated within a depth (tier block first, then minq block). The notebook plan said "alternated".
- The tier 30k prefill (339.4) is 1.8 % below its P61 value (345.4); minq 343.0 vs 344.1.
