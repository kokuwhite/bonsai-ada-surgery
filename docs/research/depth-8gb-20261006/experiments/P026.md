## Question

Does ubatch 1024 (half the per-chunk f16 conversions over the cached context) push long-mode prefill above the floor?

## Why

P025: f16 prefill reached the floor (parity, -0.5..-1.3%). P022: ub 1024 alone gave only +2-3.5% (in-place path).

## Plan and gate

(quoted) "P26 (plan): f16 prefill + ub 1024 (half the per-chunk conversions over the cached context): long mode N=58112,
ub 512 vs 1024, P20 sequence, twice. Gate: beats the floor's prefill at 16k/32k/60k by more than the sudo1/sudo2 spread
with decode unchanged and min free >= 150 MiB during the 60k request."

## Method

p26.sh: ub1024_r1, ub512_r1, ub1024_r2, ub512_r2 through run_arm.sh (P20 sequence 4k/16k/32k/60k); sample_mem.ps1
every ~3 s for the whole run (shared, dedicated and free).

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- The interim was written before ub 1024 r2's 60k step; r2 at 60k (174.8) is not in the notebook. It is also a cliff
  value (below ub 512's 252.7).
- With ub 1024 the server re-evaluates 512 more tokens per step (13,016 vs 12,504 at 16k; 17,036 vs 16,524 at 32k;
  28,937 vs 28,425 at 60k). The prefill rates therefore cover slightly different token ranges than the floor's.

## Verdict

"ub 1024 + f16 prefill beats the floor to 32k; cliff at 60k for lack of headroom." Gate FAIL at 60k.
P27 plan: "ub 1024 + f16 cap 65536 at N=53248 (~390 MiB free at load), P20 sequence x2. Gate: prefill above the floor
at all four depths, decode above the floor at all four, no one-token answers, min free >= 100 MiB during the 60k
request."

## Corrections later

None; P027 resolved the 60k cliff with a smaller N.

## What it changed

## Toolbox

- Method: trace free VRAM during the deepest request, not only at load; a transient buffer (here the f16 copy, ~240 MiB
  at 60k) plus a larger ubatch can push the process into the prefill cliff.
- Finding: on Turing, ub 1024 adds ~+2-3% prefill with f16 prefill at 4k-32k and costs +262 MiB at load.
