## Question

Is the 157/164 of P31 caused by q4_0 K/V, or is it drafting / Turing rounding noise?

## Why

## Plan and gate (as frozen before the result)

"Same harness, drafting mode with q8_0 K/V (prompts are short; positions in VRAM do not matter for this test).
Reading decided now: q8_0 >= 160 -> q4_0 costs ~3 problems: the 8 GB preset documents a BONSAI_CTK=q8_0 quality mode
with its position cost; q8_0 <= 158 -> the gap is drafting / Turing rounding noise, q4_0 stays."

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Method: two pre-registered reading branches with a gap between them. A result in the gap gets a cost/benefit
  decision, written down, not a re-run until it falls on one side.
- Model-specific: q8_0 vs q4_0 K/V = 159 vs 157 here; q8_0 halves the positions that fit in VRAM.
- Transfers: K/V quantization is a quality lever with a large VRAM price on 8 GB cards; measure both, keep the cheap
  one as default when the gap is inside noise, and document the other as an option.
