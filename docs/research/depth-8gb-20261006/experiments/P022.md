## Question

Does a packed 1-bit KQ mask free the f16 mask's VRAM (128 MiB at 131k, ub 512) without changing outputs, and does it
make ub 1024 affordable for prefill?

## Why

The f16 KQ mask is reserved at the full context (131072 x 512 x 2 B = 128 MiB), worth ~7.3k q4_0 positions.

## Plan and gate

P22 (quoted): "Arms after P20 (stock + fan 100%): test-backend-ops FLASH_ATTN_EXT on sm_75; drafting default (N=47360)
packed vs not: server VRAM at load, greedy texts at 4k/16k byte-identical (the mask is the same -inf/0 pattern); then N
resized to the freed VRAM, depth sweep; long mode at ub 1024 + packed vs ub 512: prefill and VRAM.
Gates: identical texts; test-backend-ops pass; saving >= 100 MiB; ub 1024 prefill gain > 10% at 4k and 32k to be
adopted."
P22b (quoted): "bin-p22 rebuilt with "speculative: keep the f16 KQ mask in the MTP draft context" (prism-p22 2a558c87f).
Drafting packed N=51456 sweep 4k-60k; texts at 32k packed (fixed) vs unpacked with drafting. Gates: 32k/60k decode
within 2% of RC (43.1 / 29.5) at +4,096 positions; acceptance >= 0.78; texts identical."

## Method

P22: test-backend-ops; dr_unpacked and dr_packed at N=47360 (4k quick_tps, server VRAM); re-serve each and save texts
with memclock_probe at 4k and 16k (1 rep, 400 tokens); solve N for the freed VRAM (51456); dr_packed_n51456 sweep
4k/16k/32k/60k; long mode packed at ub 512 and ub 1024 (4k, 32k).
P22b: dr_packed_fix_n51456 sweep; then texts at 32k with depth_text_probe (200 tokens) on unpacked and packed servers at
N=47360.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- P22b ran with prefetch off (PCT=0) while P22 ran with MAX_KB=1024; P017 had shown prefetch does not change values.
- The P22 identity check covered 4k and 16k only; the corruption sat past 16k and was found by P22b.

## Verdict

P22: "packed mask exact and -128 MiB, but breaks the draft context past its window; ub 1024 no."
P22b: "REJECTED - the packed KQ mask corrupts the target's output at 32k (repeated "/")." "P22's identity check (4k,
16k) passed; the break lies between 16k and 32k. The draft collapse was a symptom (a draft cannot predict a corrupted
target). My draft-window explanation was wrong; the committed draft-context change does not fix it (kept on the dev
branch only, harmless). Repeated-slash corruption is the symptom PrismML #189 reports for a quantized-V MMA path."
"Decision: packed mask out of every 8 GB configuration. Finding for Cary: the same two commits sit behind
--kq-mask-packed in llama.cpp-ada-mirai; whether Mirai corrupts at depth is unmeasured."

## Corrections later

- P024 (same day): packed vs unpacked without drafting (long mode) are byte-identical at 20k, 24k, 28k and 32k. "P22b's
  "target attention breaks at depth" was too broad - corrected here." Corruption needs drafting + packed past 16k.
- P082 (2026-10-09, outside this range): code read found the packed path matches the f16 path; the packed mask is used
  only for batches >= 32 queries (prefill), so verify/decode keep f16. "The P22b corruption has two better explanations,
  both from that build and both fixed since: (1) staging buffers keyed by slot only, shared by the target and the MTP
  draft context on different streams (today: keyed by cache, "kv<ptr>"); (2) the shared CUDA pool race (patch 0045;
  preset pool off). P22b ran with the pool on and the old staging key." P082 result: with drafting + lookup, 11/11 +
  11/11 texts identical at 32k and 64k, +108 MiB free at load, FLASH_ATTN_EXT 2994/2994. "The P22b corruption was the
  old build's races (shared staging key / pool), not the packed mask - P22b/P24 conclusions corrected here."

## What it changed

At the time: the packed mask stayed out of the RC and RC2. The ub 1024 idea came back with f16 prefill in P026/P027.
Later (P082-P084): the packed mask returned on the newer engine.

## Toolbox

- Method: a mask format change must be text-tested at every depth band the model will run (here the break was between
  16k and 32k), with and without drafting.
- Lesson: when a VRAM saving and a correctness failure appear together, test the saving alone in a configuration without
  shared components (no drafting, no pool) before blaming it. Here the real cause was two cross-stream races in the
  same build.
- Finding: the f16 mask is n_ctx x ubatch x 2 B; the packed mask cut server VRAM by 128 MiB at load at 131k / ub 512
  (P022); ub 1024 alone gave only +2-3.5% prefill on Turing.
- Model-specific: none; the mask is generic attention plumbing.
