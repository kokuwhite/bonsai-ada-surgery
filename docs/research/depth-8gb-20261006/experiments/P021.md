## Question

Is the 12 GB default (tail 4) exposed to the one-token bug? How confident is the 8 GB default (tail 1)? Does turning the
RAM prompt cache off remove the failure on tail 2 (restore hypothesis)?

## Why

P018: tail 2 failed 3/27 after a prompt-cache restore; the 12 GB default is draft 2 / tail 4 (five planes).

## Plan and gate

(quoted from the P18 result) "P21 (plan, runs during P20's CPU-only build): same sequence, x3 tail 4 (N=20224, fits the
same budget), x3 tail 1 (confidence on the default), x2 tail 2 with LLAMA_ARG_CACHE_RAM=0 (prompt cache off: tests the
restore hypothesis)." The reading: count one-token answers per arm.

## Method

p21.sh: t4_r1, t1_r1, t4_r2, t1_r2, t4_r3, t1_r3, then t2nocache_r1, t2nocache_r2; quick_tps 4k -> 16k -> 32k per
replay (9 answers).

t4_r2 0 of 9; t4_r3 0 of 0.

With the cache off, the code prompt evaluated the whole filler at each depth (4,048 / 16,036 / 32,044 tokens) instead of
the incremental part (12,504 / 16,524 with the cache on). Tail 4 at 32k decodes 23.4 because N=20224 puts 32k past the
VRAM line. Decode values are depressed by the concurrent builds (e.g. t4_r1 4k 47.6 vs ~52 unloaded).

Notebook: "Tail 1: 3 replays, 0/27. Tail 4 (N=20224): 2 replays, 0/18. Tail 2 with LLAMA_ARG_CACHE_RAM=0: 1 replay,
0/9. Overall tail 2: failed 2 of 6 completed runs (P13, P18 r3). The restore hypothesis is neither confirmed nor refuted
(one cache-off replay is not enough at this rate). Status: open, intermittent, reproducible with P13's sequence; drafted
default stays tail 1 (0 of 54 answers at 32k across P15/P18/P21). Handed to Cary with the replay script."

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- Stock guard refusals: "three arms (t4_r3, t2nocache_r1) were refused by the stock guard (card read 215 W after four
  apply retries - Afterburner unreliable under full CPU load)." The notebook says three arms and names two; the receipts
  hold two refused arms (t4_r3.txt and t2nocache_r1.txt, 53 bytes each).
- "0 of 54 answers at 32k across P15/P18/P21": 54 is the count of all tail-1 answers at 4k/16k/32k in P018 (27) and
  P021 (27). The 32k-only count is 18 (plus 3 in P015). P029's running total ("0 of 77") continues the all-depths count.

## Verdict

"tail 1 0/27 again (0/54 today); tail 4 0/18; tail 2 cache-off 0/9 - the bug is rare." The drafted default stays tail 1.

## Corrections later

  81 answers. "The P18 failures (2026-10-07) ran with GGML_CUDA_SHARED_POOL=1 (bin-p12 C2 config). Most likely the same
  pool race (fixed: pool off in the preset, patch 0045)."

## What it changed

Confidence in tail 1 as the drafted default; the replay script went to Cary.

## Toolbox

- Method: for a rare bug, count failures per arm over many replays and say what the count can and cannot rule out (one
  cache-off replay cannot refute a ~1-in-3-runs failure).
- Lesson (rig): Afterburner profile applies are unreliable under full CPU load; keep the stock guard and record
  refusals as void arms.
- Model-specific: tail depth sets the number of 149.6 MiB GDN rollback planes; tail 4 costs ~27k q4_0 positions vs
  tail 1 on this card.
