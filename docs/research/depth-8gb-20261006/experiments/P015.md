## Question

C2 runs draft 1 / tail 2. Does tail 1 free one recurrent-state plane, and do the extra positions beat tail 2 at depth?

## Why

Code (common.h:403, server-context.cpp:991): n_rs_seq = max(draft n_max, n_max_tail); the GDN rollback ring is sized for
the whole session from it. With draft 1 / tail 2, one recurrent-state plane exists only for drafting past the VRAM line.

## Plan and gate

"Arms on bin-p12 (q4 head, pool, MAX_KB=1024): C2 tail 2 vs tail 1 at N=38400 with LLAMA_LOG_VERBOSITY=5 (buffer
breakdown), server VRAM at load; then tail 1 resized to ~257 MiB free and swept 4k/16k/32k/60k.
Gates: tail 1 frees >= 100 MiB; at its larger N it beats C2-tail-2 at 32k and at 60k by more than the sudo1/sudo2
spread, without losing >2% at 4k/16k. Then it is measured against P13's sudo floor at 60k."

## Method

run_arm.sh: tail 2 and tail 1 at N=38400 (4k only), server VRAM = used - idle; N for tail 1 solved for ~257 MiB free
from the tail 1 fixed cost; then tail 1 at that N through quick_tps 4k/16k/32k/60k.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- The 60k comparison uses P012's tail 2 number 23.9, which P012 measured at 64k (its sweep was 4k/16k/32k/64k), not
  60k. The notebook writes it in the 60k column. P013's tail 2 at 60k was VOID (one-token answers). So the "+24% at 60k"
  gate compares tail 1 at 60k with tail 2 at 64k.
- Note in the result: "this run used MAX_KB=1024 with drafting and produced full answers at 32k and 60k - the P13
  one-token stop is not "prefetch + drafting at depth" in general; P17 retests P13's exact C2 (tail 2) configuration."

## What it changed

The 8 GB drafting mode: draft 1 / tail 1 (launcher: "8 GB: tail 1. The recurrent-state rollback ring is sized max(draft,
tail); tail 2 holds one more 150 MiB plane."), N=47,360 at 1280x1024.

## Toolbox

- Method: read how the engine sizes per-session state (here n_rs_seq = max(draft, tail)) and remove planes you do not
  use; measure the saving with a verbose buffer log.
- Model-specific: Bonsai 27B's 48 GDN layers hold recurrent state; each rollback plane costs 149.62 MiB on this build,
  ~8-10k q4_0 positions on 8 GB. Pure-attention models have no such plane; other hybrids (Mamba/GDN) do.
- Transfers: on small cards a drafted mode should keep only the rollback depth it uses below the VRAM line.
