## Question

With the 4070's candidate build (d6c6e9f01, 64-cell tile map), is sparse decode equal to dense on quality at 160k
and 245k on 8 GB, and faster? Does MTP tail 4 (5-query verify ops on the MMA kernel) give the 4070's speed path on
8 GB?

## Why

4070 (#21, 08:18Z), 160k, line 32,768 (~127k host), d6c6e9f01, prefill dense, decode sparse B=8192: "dense 13.6
tok/s 10/10 12/12; page 256 31.1 tok/s 9/10 12/12 (misses _div_nearest twice); page 64 27.6 tok/s (code item 31.7)
10/10 12/12. FLASH_ATTN_EXT 3007/3007." P102e on the same build: sync gone, but 245k sparse 4.3 vs dense 5.7.

## Plan and gate

Notebook: "P105 (their "P104"; renamed here) on the 2060, same build: dense vs sparse at 160k and 245k: code copies
(10), needles (12), decode (quick_tps at 160k; 245k speed from P102e and the code items); sync-warning count (expect
0) and fast: line. Gate: copies and needles equal to dense at each depth; then decode decides. Order: P102e (running)
-> P103b (bundle blocker) -> P105 -> P104 (head calibration)."
Amendment (P102e result): "P105 amended: + speed arms at 160k with MTP tail 4 (5-query verify -> MMA 64-cell tile
map), sparse and dense, margin 675 (3 more rollback planes)."

## Method

- For depth D in 160000, 245000; arm in dense, sparse: one server for code_needle.py (10 exact copies) and one for
  needle_depth.py (12 facts). Notes per server: count of "one sync per K write" and the first "fast:" line.
- Speed: run_arm.sh quick_tps at 160000 for dense, sparse, sparse tail 4, dense tail 4.

## What it changed

First real 8 GB speed-up for sparse reads (1.9x at 160k). docs/DEPTH.md quality and speed rows; docs/8GB.md "+68 %
decode at 160k on code items (12.8 -> 21.5 tok/s), not yet faster at 245k". Tail 4 ruled out on 8 GB. P106 next.

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Lever: sparse host-tail reads with a 64-cell tile map; 1.9x decode at 160k on an 8 GB PCIe 3.0 card, quality equal
  at 160k.
- Model-specific: MTP tail 4 needs three more recurrent-state rollback planes (~150 MiB each for this model's 48 GDN
  layers), which cost 25,600 cells of VRAM line and drop acceptance 0.79 -> 0.55 here.
- Transfers: test the sparse path on the decode op shape the target card really runs; a speed-up at mid depth can
  vanish at the deepest point when per-page costs grow with the tail (1,741 pages at 160k, 3,077 at 245k).
