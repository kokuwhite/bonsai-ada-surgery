## Question

Does the 8 GB preset in the launcher pick the measured modes and size the VRAM line by itself, with no tuning
variables?

## Why

P26-P29 found two modes by hand (drafting and long context). The preset must reproduce them for users.

## Plan and gate (as frozen before the result)

"Gates: the printed preset matches the measured modes (q4_0, 131k, draft 1 / tail 1, ub 512 / 1024, f16 32768 /
65536); auto-sized N within 2,048 of the measured 47,360 / 53,248; free VRAM at load >= 150 MiB; decode at 4k and 32k
within 3% of P28; no one-token answers."

## Method

run_arm.sh per arm through the launcher copy; quick_tps at 4k and 32k (the 32k step extends the 4k prompt).

## Results

| drafting (auto) | 53,760 | 225 | 7,707 / 7,439 | 292 | 150 | 50.9 | 43.2 | 408.0 | 329.9 | 0.798 (8) |
| long (plain file) | 58,880 | 400 | 7,627 / 7,359 | 380 | 230 | 43.9 | 34.2 | 432.4 | 353.3 | - |

Per prompt decode, drafting: 4k code 51.2 / prose 50.1 / bash 51.5; 32k 43.0 / 43.1 / 43.5. Long: 4k 43.9 x3;
32k 34.3 / 34.2 / 34.1. Long mode used ub 1024 and f16 cap 65536 (printed by the launcher, per the notebook).

P28 reference: drafting 51.2 (4k) / 43.1 (32k); long 43.7 / 34.3.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

The idle desktop used 268 MiB this time, ~120 MiB less than the night before. The auto-sizing followed it.

## Verdict

Notebook: "Gates: preset printed as measured PASS; free >= 150 PASS; decode within 3% of P28 PASS; one-token 0 PASS.
"N within 2,048 of 47,360 / 53,248" FAIL as written - the sizing followed the 120 MiB lighter desktop, which is its
job (noted, not re-gated). Test copy of the launcher removed."

Note: "min free 150" is the reading at the end of the arm (run_arm.sh reads free VRAM once at the end).

## Corrections later

The 2026-10-07 correction (events file) found that branch launchers ignore `BONSAI_BIN`. It states: "P34 is not
affected (its copy was edited to rc2)". The 43.9 long-mode number here became the reference that exposed the
wrong-engine runs in P40.

## What it changed

The preset table of PR #6 and the pre-release bundle-20261007 notes use these numbers (drafting 50.9 / 43.2,
408 / 330; long 43.9 / 34.2, 432 / 353; 53,760 and 58,880 positions).

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Method: run the user path (launcher, no variables) and gate it against the hand-tuned receipts.
- Transfers: a VRAM-line gate written as "N within X of a fixed number" is wrong when the sizing is dynamic; gate on
  free VRAM at load and on speed instead. Record idle desktop VRAM in every receipt; on a card that also drives the
  desktop, it moves by 100+ MiB between sessions.
