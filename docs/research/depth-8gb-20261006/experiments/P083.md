## Question

With patch 0045, is the shared pool (+ draft ubatch 256) safe on 8 GB again, and does its saving buy decode at depth?

## Why

Notebook: "P12 (old engine): pool + DRAFT_UBATCH 256 saved 62 MiB here, then caused the 1-token race (fixed by 0045, in
bin-0046). 12 GB now ships it (+1.3k line, decode +4 % at 131k)."

## Plan and gate

Notebook: "Arms (bin-0046, 131k, drafting preset): A preset (N 50,176); P pool on + DRAFT_UBATCH 256, N 52,224 (+2,048
= 36 MiB of the 62); sweep 4k/32k/64k/96k with VRAM trace; then fresh 60k x3 with P (the old 1-token repro). Gates: 0
one-token; trace min free >= 60 MiB; P decode >= A - 1 % at 4k/32k and >= A at 64k/96k. Pass -> 8 GB preset PR (pool on
with 0046 engines, line +2,048)."

## Method

A sweep, P sweep (one server each, quick_tps 4k/32k/64k/96k, trace every 10 s), then 3 servers with P, fresh 60k each.

Gates pass: 0 one-token; trace min 131 MiB (>= 60); P vs A: 4k -0.2 % (>= A - 1 %), 32k equal, 64k +7 %, 96k +4 %.

## Toolbox

- Lever: one CUDA memory pool shared by the target and the MTP draft context (+ a smaller draft ubatch) saves ~51 MiB
  of fixed cost here = ~2-3k more KV cells in VRAM = +4-7 % decode at 64k-96k.
- Precondition: cross-stream ordering (patch 0045); without it, the race of P64.
- Method: re-test a reverted feature after its fix with the same repro that found the bug (fresh 60k x3).
