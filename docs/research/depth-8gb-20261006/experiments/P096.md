## Question

Does the published bundle-20261010 zip pass the 8 GB release gates on the RTX 2060 SUPER?

## Why

bundle-20261010 was published by the 4070 session with patches 0050-0052 and PR #18. The 4070 also explained the
160k shlex quote difference: "the 160k shlex quote is a near-tie the chunk tips, and the chunked answer is the exact
copy of the source (unchunked adds a blank line)". Every bundle gets a release check on the 2060 (as P88 for
bundle-20261009-vram).

## Plan and gate

Notebook: "P96 = P88 with the new zip and main's launcher (chunk on for 8 GB, RAM tiers): tests, sweep 4k-128k with
trace, fresh 60k x2, lookup d0 + d64k. Gates: sha; tests; launcher lines (window 262144, chunks of 32768, ram line);
decode within 2 % of P85/P91 (N permitting); 0 one-token; trace min >= 60."

## Method

- Download the zip (gh release download), check sha256, unpack.
- test-backend-ops -b CUDA0 for ADD, RMS_NORM, FLASH_ATTN_EXT.
- run_arm.sh sweep: quick_tps.py 4000 -> 32000 -> 64000 -> 96000 -> 128000 with a free-VRAM trace every 10 s.
- Two fresh servers with one 60k prompt each.
- One server: lookup_ab.py depth 0 and 64000 (22 items).
