## Question

Does the packed mask corrupt output without drafting, and if so, at which depth does it start?

## Why

P022b: packed + drafting wrote "////..." at 32k; P022 had passed at 4k and 16k. "P24 (plan): long mode (no head) bin-p22,
packed vs unpacked, texts at 24k and 32k, to place the threshold without drafting in the picture."

## Plan and gate

(quoted from the P22b result) "long mode (no head) bin-p22, packed vs unpacked, texts at 24k and 32k, to place the
threshold without drafting in the picture." Reading: identical texts at all depths -> the corruption needs drafting.

## Method

One server per arm (unpacked 04:34:31, packed 04:42:42). depth_text_probe.py at 20000, 24000, 28000, 32000: two prompts
(merge intervals code; heat pump explanation), 60 tokens each, greedy. Texts compared per depth and prompt.

## Results

| depth | unpacked decode (code / prose) | packed decode (code / prose) | texts packed vs unpacked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20k | 35.1 / 35.3 | 34.1 / 34.2 | identical, identical |
| 24k | 33.1 / 32.6 | 32.9 / 32.7 | identical, identical |
| 28k | 32.9 / 33.0 | 34.7 / 34.0 | identical, identical |
| 32k | 32.0 / 31.8 | 33.3 / 33.3 | identical, identical |

All 16 answers: finish=length, 60 tokens. Example: the 32k prose text starts "Here is a step-by-step explanation of how a
heat pump works in winter" in both arms; at 20k it starts "A heat pump works in winter by reversing the standard cooling
cycle". (Decode values are over 60 tokens; not a speed test.)

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- The run added 20k and 28k to the planned 24k and 32k.
- scripts/p24.sh holds `sed 's#\#/#g; ...'` with one backslash lost (the same defect as p11b.sh); the readiness wait
  cannot read the server log path and runs to its 300 s limit before the first request. The receipts show complete runs.

## Verdict

"the packed mask is exact WITHOUT drafting; it corrupts only combined with MTP drafting." "With P22b: corruption needs
drafting + packed past 16k. P22b's "target attention breaks at depth" was too broad - corrected here. Unverified lead:
with n_rs_seq > 0 the target splits ubatches with split_equal (rollback window kept together); the packed mask's
set_input path may not handle that layout. Same two commits are opt-in on the Mirai engine, which drafts with MTP: worth
a 32k drafting check there."
"Product: packed mask is a candidate for the long-context (no-head) mode only (+128 MiB: ~+7.4k positions or a wider
margin against the prefill cliff) - needs a launcher guard (never with BONSAI_SPEC > 0). Cary's call; not in the RC."

## Corrections later

- P082 (2026-10-09, outside this range): the split_equal lead was not the cause. "The P22b corruption was the old
  build's races (shared staging key / pool), not the packed mask - P22b/P24 conclusions corrected here." With patch
  0045 and cache-keyed staging, packed + drafting passed 22/22 texts at 32k and 64k.

## What it changed

The packed mask stayed out of RC2. It came back on the 2026-10-09 engine (P082-P084, outside this range).

## Toolbox

- Method: to place a depth threshold for a correctness break, run short (60-token) greedy texts at a ladder of depths in
  both arms; remove one suspect component (here drafting) per run.
- Lesson: "exact without drafting, wrong with drafting" points at the interaction (cross-stream sharing, ubatch
  layout), not at either component alone.
