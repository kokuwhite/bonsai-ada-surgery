## Question

Can prefill use sudo's faster f16-converted flash attention without reserving f16 K/V for the whole window, by taking
the f16 copy from the CUDA pool at the actual KV length for prefill-sized batches only?

## Why

P023: our incremental prefill at depth is -15% (32k) / -21% (60k) below the floor. Cause (code + P016b): his fork
converts q4_0 K/V to f16 per chunk and runs f16 MMA; ours dequantizes in the kernel; the f16 route was avoided because
its buffer was reserved at load for the full window (512 MiB at 131k).

## Plan and gate

(quoted from P023) "P25 (plan): f16 conversion for prefill-sized batches only (Q columns > 8), the buffer taken from the
CUDA pool at the actual KV length (128 MiB at 32k, 240 MiB at 60k) instead of reserved; decode keeps the in-place path.
Env switch GGML_CUDA_FA_PREFILL_F16=1, worktree off the release candidate. Gates: texts identical to in-place at 4k/32k;
prefill gain > 10% at 32k and 60k (P20 sequence) without a cliff at the long mode's N; decode unchanged."

## Method

p25.sh: (1) test-backend-ops -o FLASH_ATTN_EXT -b CUDA0 with the switch forcing the path; (2) texts at a fresh 32k
(depth_text_probe, 120 tokens, two prompts), long mode N=58112, in-place vs F16=65536; (3) quick_tps P20 sequence
(4k/16k/32k/60k) lc_native, lc_f16, dr_native (4k/16k/32k), dr_f16_32k, lc_f16_r2.

## Toolbox

- Method: get the fast kernel's layout (f16 K/V) without its fixed VRAM cost by allocating the conversion buffer from the
  pool at the actual KV length, only for prefill-sized batches; decode keeps the in-place quantized read.
- Finding: on Turing, f16 MMA over converted K/V beats in-kernel q4_0 dequant for prefill by +18% at 32k and +26% at 60k
  (incremental prefill); the gain grows with depth.
- Lesson: the transient buffer (128 MiB at 32k, 240 MiB at 60k) needs headroom; cap the depth where the path is used by
  the free VRAM of the mode.
- Transfers: any card and any quantized-KV model with flash attention; the cap is card-specific.
