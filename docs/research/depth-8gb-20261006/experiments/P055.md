## Question

Which kernels launch per steady decode step on the newest engine, for plain decode and for the drafting step? This is
the next fusion target list.

## Why

Backlog item 7: "kernel census on bin-lookup (dev stats build needed: rebuild prism-p37 rebased on 008ac1112) - next
fusion targets."

## Plan and gate (as frozen before the result)

"Build prism-p55 (sm_75) -> tooling/build-p55. Raw server, plain model, -c 8192 q4_0, GGML_CUDA_GRAPH_STATS=1,
verbosity 5, 64-token decode; then the MTP head model with --spec-type draft-mtp --spec-draft-n-max 1 (the drafting
step). Output: up to 6 distinct kernel mixes per run. Reading only: the fusion target list for steady decode."

## Method

Build, then per model: start the raw server, one 64-token greedy completion, grep "cuda graph stats" (60 lines after
each) from the server's stderr.
