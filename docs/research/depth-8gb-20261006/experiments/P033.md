## Question

Cary: DFlash "allows higher draft count and reduces VRAM". Measure before believing: does DFlash 2 beat our MTP head
(draft 1) on this card at a VRAM cost an 8 GB card can pay?

## Why

Published Bonsai drafters: Schiltmans DFlash2-ft5 GGUF (Q8_0 2.06 GB, Q4_K_M 1.14 GB, Q2_K 0.71 GB; needs
prism-b10754+; M4 Pro: acceptance 0.43, slower than no draft), ProCreations DFlash2 (acceptance 0.50 at 3 drafts on
Blackwell, no MTP comparison). Our MTP head: 0.2 GB file, acceptance 0.80 at draft 1 (P28). Concern: Bonsai's
rollback ring is sized max(draft) at ~150 MiB/step.

## Plan and gate (as frozen before the result)

"Arms (PrismML's own Windows build prism-b10754-2459f68 cuda-13.3; no tiered cache there, so a 16k window all in
VRAM; stock + fan 100%): plain (no draft) / DFlash Q2_K n-max 3 / DFlash Q2_K n-max 7 / DFlash Q4_K_M n-max 3 if it
fits. Same quick_tps probes at 4k and 12k; server VRAM at load; acceptance from the log.
Gates, written before the run: DFlash is worth porting only if its decode at 4k AND 12k beats our drafting mode at the
same depths (51.2 / ~48) on this card, at a VRAM cost that leaves >= 32k positions in VRAM. Otherwise documented as
measured and dropped for 8 GB."

## Method

run_arm.sh per arm (stop, verify stock, serve raw command, quick_tps at 4000 and 12000, acceptance from the server
log, stop). Order: plain, Q2_K n3, Q2_K n7, Q4_K_M n3. quick_tps: code / prose / bash prompts, 400 tokens, greedy,
thinking off.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- First attempt (scripts/p33.sh, 10:07): the official release `prism-b10754-2459f68 win-cuda-13.3-x64` crashed right
  after buffer reservation with a bare `ggml-cuda.cu:107: CUDA error` (plain arm, no drafter; receipt
  FAILED-release-cu133-plain.txt; p33-run.out shows plain and dflash_q2_n3 "did not start").
- Diagnosis (notebook): ggml-cuda.dll holds sm_86 / sm_89 / sm_120 code and no sm_75. release-prism.yml sets no
  CMAKE_CUDA_ARCHITECTURES, so Turing gets only 75-virtual PTX; the 13.3 zip's PTX cannot JIT on driver 591.86
  (CUDA 13.1). The 12.4 zip loads (~2 min first-start JIT) and answers 17*23 = 391. Every RTX 20 card fails with
  the 13.3 release. Issue filed after a duplicate check: PrismML-Eng/llama.cpp#332.
- Reroute: bin-prism (prism 6bfcd79 + Turing fix, sm_75), scripts/p33b.sh.

## Verdict

Notebook: "Gate: beats ours at 4k and 12k PASS (Q2_K n3: +8% / +4%); leaves >= 32k positions FAIL - with a 16k
window it already uses our drafting mode's whole budget; the drafter setup costs ~645 MiB more than the MTP head
(~38k q4_0 positions); prefill -33%. n7 ("more drafts") halves decode on this card (acceptance 0.34). Q4_K_M runs
into memory pressure. Decision: not for 8 GB. Candidate for 12 GB+ (where 645 MiB is affordable) - receipt for Cary."

Receipt check on the speed half of the gate: the PASS holds on the code prompt only (55.5 vs 51.3 code at 4k). On the
three-prompt mean, DFlash Q2_K n3 is below our drafting mode (49.1 vs 51.2 at 4k; 45.6 at 12k vs 47.5 at 16k). The
12k DFlash depth was compared with our 16k number. The final decision (reject for 8 GB) does not change: the VRAM
half fails in every reading.

## Corrections later

None in the notebook.

## What it changed

DFlash dropped for 8 GB. Issue #332 filed on PrismML. Result kept as a 12 GB+ candidate.

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Method: run a foreign drafter on its own engine with a raw command line, same probe, and compare decode, prefill,
  acceptance and VRAM at load against the shipped mode.
- Model-specific: DFlash2-ft5 drafters for Bonsai 27B; acceptance 0.56 (Q2_K, n3) vs 0.80 for the MTP head at draft 1.
- Transfers: a separate drafter model costs VRAM for its weights and its own KV; on 8 GB that buys fewer positions
  than it saves time. More draft tokens with low acceptance (n7, 0.34) halve decode. Check that an official binary
  carries code for your GPU arch (sm_75) before you measure it; PTX for a newer CUDA than the driver fails with a bare
  CUDA error. Compare means with means.
