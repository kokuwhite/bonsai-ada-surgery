## Question

With fewer positions in VRAM (53,248) and so more headroom for the 60k transient, does the ub 1024 + f16 long mode beat
the floor at all four depths?

## Why

P026: ub 1024 + f16 beat the floor to 32k but hit a pressure cliff at 60k (trace min 83 MiB).

## Plan and gate

(quoted) "P27 (plan): ub 1024 + f16 cap 65536 at N=53248 (~390 MiB free at load), P20 sequence x2. Gate: prefill above
the floor at all four depths, decode above the floor at all four, no one-token answers, min free >= 100 MiB during the
60k request."

## Method

Two runs (r1 05:58:17, r2 06:05:13) through run_arm.sh at 4k/16k/32k/60k; sample_mem.ps1 every ~3 s.

## Results

Server 7,240 MiB, free 378 at load (both runs); 118 at the end of each run; trace minimum 118 MiB.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- Decode at 60k is lower than at N=58112 (22.4 vs 25.4 in P026) because 60k is further past the VRAM line at 53,248
  ("past ~53k decode reads the host tail").
- As in P026, ub 1024 re-evaluates 512 more tokens per step than the floor's ub 512 sequence.

## Verdict

"long mode with ub 1024 + f16 prefill at 53,248 positions meets the floor everywhere" (60k prefill: a tie). "Long-context
mode candidate: no head, N=53248, ub 1024, f16 prefill cap 65536 (window 131k; past ~53k decode reads the host tail).
Drafting mode keeps ub 512, f16 cap 32768: prefill -3.4..-3.7% below the floor at 4k-32k, decode +39..+76%."
"RC2 build (sm_75/86/89 + PTX) from prism-p25 started 06:2x -> bin-rc2."

(auto-sized by the preset later).

## Toolbox

- Method: trade positions in VRAM for transient headroom when a prefill path needs a depth-proportional buffer; gate on
  the trace minimum during the deepest request.
- Finding: on this card, 1 MiB of headroom costs ~57 q4_0 positions; 53,248 positions with ~378 MiB free at load kept the
  60k prefill above 100 MiB free.
