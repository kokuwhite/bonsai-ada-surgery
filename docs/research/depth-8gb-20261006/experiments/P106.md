## Question

Does the vec-kernel warp skip (028f606fc) make 2-query sparse decode faster at 245k on 8 GB? Where does the
512-token follow-up prefill loss with sparse on come from (P102e, P105)? What does sparse cost at a small host tail?

## Why

P105: 160k sparse 1.9x dense, but 245k not faster, and the 512-token follow-up prefill dropped 106 -> 85 at 160k (P102e:
79 -> 39 at 245k). #21 (4070, 12:32Z): "028f606fc: the vec kernel skips a warp's 32 cells when all are masked
(2-query decode at page 64). Prefill halving: not seen on the 4070 (296 vs 284 at 160k); 8 GB-specific."

## Plan and gate

Notebook: "P106 (queue 69d, built in P105's next prefill): FLASH_ATTN_EXT tests; arms (a) sparse + OP_TIMING, (b)
no FA_SPARSE (FAST + STAGE_MIN_Q kept), (c) sparse + NOTRACK, each 64k -> 160k; then 245k decode sparse lookup off."
No numeric gate (diagnosis + speed).

## Method

test-backend-ops -o FLASH_ATTN_EXT -b CUDA0. Then run_arm.sh per arm: quick_tps 64000 -> 160000 (arms a, b, c), and
245000 (decode245_sparse). Arm a logs per-op timing (GGML_CUDA_OP_TIMING=1: "op timing over 100 graphs").

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- Arm a runs with OP_TIMING, which slows decode (18.9 / 14.6 vs arm c 29.0 / 18.5); the notebook reads sparse speed
  from arm c.

## Verdict

Notebook: "028f606fc: 160k 1.9x again; 245k 4.9 (dense 5.7); sparse costs 16 % at 64k; prefill cost is not tracking
... 512-token follow-up at 160k: a 84, b 107, c 85; at 64k ~193-200 all. Cold 95k-token prefill equal (147-148). ->
the follow-up prefill cost comes with FA_SPARSE set, not from write tracking. Sparse costs -16 % at 64k (host tail
~18k): needs a depth threshold to ship. 245k decode sparse 4.9 (d6c6e9f01: 4.3; dense 5.7). Op timing at 160k (arm
a): FLASH_ATTN_EXT 28.5 of 57.8 ms per graph (49 %). P107 (queue 69e, before P104): OP_TIMING dense vs sparse at 245k
-> which op grows with the host tail."

## Corrections later

P107 and P111: the sparse arms here over-committed VRAM at the first sparse decode step (margin 225; the sparse tables
were not in the budget). The 245k reading (4.9 vs 5.7) and the follow-up prefill drop (107 -> 85 at 160k) are that
over-commit, not the sparse path. On the 8d5b862fc path with margin 475: 245k 11.6 vs 5.2 (P111, OP_TIMING on), 160k
19.6 vs 9.1 clean (P112), follow-ups equal to dense. The -16 % at 64k is mostly an always-on FA_SPARSE cost
(P112: -20 % with no sparse op run; P115 open).

## What it changed

docs/DEPTH.md (PR #26): 8 GB speed rows 64k 34.5 / 29.0 (-16 %), 160k 9.7 / 18.5 (x1.9), 245k 5.7 / 4.9 for 028f606fc,
and the three open items (245k below dense; follow-up prefill -20/-45 %; -16 % at 64k). Status line: "On a real 8 GB
card it is ~1.9x at 160k, not yet faster at 245k, and it costs ~16 % at 64k and 20-45 % on follow-up prefill at
depth."

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Method: split a regression by toggling one switch at a time (sparse set / unset / tracking off) on the same build,
  same depths; then op timing on the slow arm.
- Model-specific: at 160k with sparse on, the 16 attention ops still take ~49 % of a decode graph on this card.
- Transfers: a sparse read path needs a depth threshold (it costs time when the offloaded tail is small) and must not
  slow the small follow-up prefills of agent turns.
