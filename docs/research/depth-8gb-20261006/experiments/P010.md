## Question

Do the open PrismML PRs #209 (fusion) and #314 (L2 prefetch of the next weights) speed up decode on Turing, and what
prefetch size suits a 4 MB L2?

## Why

"Free alpha from open PrismML PRs, tuned for Turing." #314's defaults (50% of the next weights, <= 16 MiB, last 46 CTAs)
were fitted to the 4070's 36 MB L2; the 2060 SUPER has 4 MB of L2 and 34 SMs.

## Plan and gate

P10 arms (q4_0 B serve, same clock profile, alternating, memclock_probe 4k 2x2 + one 16k pass): bin-turing (reference) /
bin-alpha PCT=0 (= #209 alone) / bin-alpha defaults / MAX_KB=2048 / MAX_KB=1024 / MAX_KB=2048 CTAS=34. Gates written
before the run (quoted):
- "#209 alone wins if bin-alpha PCT=0 beats bin-turing by more than the run-to-run spread, with greedy texts
  byte-identical (the fusion must not change values; if texts differ, report KL before keeping it);"
- "a prefetch setting wins only if it beats bin-alpha PCT=0 by more than the spread at both 4k and 16k, texts identical
  to PCT=0 (prefetch never changes values);"
- "result goes back to #314 / #209 as a Turing receipt comment (useful upstream whichever way it lands)."

P10b plan: "After P11. bin-alpha, B serve, stock + fan 100%. Arms alternating x2: PCT=0 / defaults / MAX_KB=1024 /
MAX_KB=512. Gates: defaults below PCT=0 in both rounds at both depths (confirms the regression); MAX_KB=1024 above PCT=0
in both rounds at both depths by more than the round-to-round spread (confirms the gain); 512 reported. Texts identical."

## Method

Per arm: stop, idle check, slot 5 (175 W check), serve, memclock_probe at 4k (2 prompts x 400 x 2 reps) and at 16k
(2 prompts x 400 x 1 rep). Texts compared with a_off1 (P10) or off (P10b). P10 order: turing1, a_off1, a_def, a_kb2048,
a_kb1024, a_kb2048_c34, turing2, a_off2. P10b order: off1, def1, kb1024_1, kb512_1, off2, def2, kb1024_2, kb512_2.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- P10b ran after P011 as planned. The 16k probe has one rep per arm (not two).
- The #314 comment quotes the 1024 gain as "+1.4 / +0.6 / +2.2%" (4k) and "+1.2 / +0.7 / +2.2%" (16k): its P10 value is
  against the mean of a_off1 and a_off2, while the notebook's P10 gate used a_off1 alone (+0.73 / +0.52 tok/s). Both are
  in the receipts; they use different baselines.

## Verdict

"#209 no effect on Turing; #314's 4070 defaults cost Turing 2.4%; sized for 4 MB L2 it gains ~1.5%." P10b: "replicated
- #314 as merged-to-be costs Turing ~2.5%; capped at 1 MiB it gains ~1.5%." "#314's final commit a4249f5 hardcodes
exactly the tested defaults (50% / 46 CTAs / 16 MiB). Comment with receipts posted to #314. Not adopting #314 into the
8 GB default build until its final form handles small L2."

## Corrections later

- P013 (C2 with MAX_KB=1024) produced one-token answers at 32k/60k; P017 cleared prefetch ("texts identical across
  prefetch on/off"); the stop was a prompt-cache-restore bug (P018), later traced to the shared CUDA pool (P064/P054,
  outside this range).
- P023: #314 (and #209) do not change prefill.
- P043 (2026-10-07, outside this range): #314's new commit e697877d9 (2 MiB cap off Ada, 1 CTA per SM) gave no gain on
  Turing (off 41.4/41.2/41.2 vs on 40.7/41.3/41.3 at 4k); posted on #314.
- 2026-10-07: #209 was taken into the bonsai patch stack as patch 0040 (PR #8) for the 12 GB session to test (outside
  this range); P056 measured patch 0040 on 8 GB at +1-2% decode, within round spread.

## What it changed

- Upstream: Turing receipt comment on #314 (small-L2 regression and a byte cap of a quarter of L2).
- Dev builds bin-p12 and bin-p22 carried #314 and #209 and used MAX_KB=1024 in some arms (P013-P022). The release
  candidates (bin-rc, bin-rc2) carry neither.

## Toolbox

- Method: cherry-pick open upstream PRs into a side worktree, keep their env switches, and screen them with the same
  probe; replicate any win in alternating rounds before shipping or posting.
- Finding that transfers: a prefetch size tuned for a large L2 (36 MB) regresses a small-L2 card (4 MB); scale the byte
  cap with `l2CacheSize` (a quarter of L2 worked here).
- Model-specific: weight prefetch for PTQ1_0 mat-vecs; fusion of ADD+RMS_NORM+MUL had no measurable effect here.
