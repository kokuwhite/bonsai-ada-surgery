## Question

P003 pointed at the core clock and heat. How much decode do more power, more fan and core offsets buy, and is the
output still exact?

## Why

P003: decode tracked the core clock as the GPU passed its 81 C target, and power touched the 175 W limit.

## Plan and gate

Arm order: stock, slot 1, 2, 3, 4, slot 1 again (drift). Same probe as P003 (4k filler in VRAM, 2 prompts x 400 x 2).
Note in the plan: "PowerLimit=123 is out of range and silently ignored; 122 is the max." Gates written before the run
(quoted):
- "identity: byte-identical to the stock arm at every step; a mismatch or a driver reset (server error) stops the
  climb, applies stock, and fails that arm;"
- "applied: slot 1 shows power.limit 215 W and fan 85%; each core arm's median core clock under load is above the
  previous arm's by at least half its offset step (else the offset did not apply and the arm is void);"
- "speed: an arm wins only if its mean decode beats the previous arm by more than the slot-1 vs slot-1-again spread."
"Afterwards: apply stock unless a winner is confirmed by a 10-minute soak in a later entry."

## Method

As P003. The script also checks `/health` after each arm (a dead server = driver reset) and stops on any mismatch.

Gates: identity PASS every arm. Applied PASS (core median +30 / +30 / +60 MHz per step, power 215 W, fan 85%). Speed:
per-step FAIL (each +50 step adds 0.24-0.52 tok/s, inside the 0.54 slot-1 drift). Cumulative +150 vs slot 1: +1.14;
vs stock: +1.82 (+4.9%). Decode scales at about half the core clock (+9.9% clock -> +4.9% decode).

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

The card heated through the sweep to 87 C (max operating 89 C) at fan 85%. Later arms are understated.

## Verdict

"+4.9% at the most, capped by heat; card left at stock." "+150 is not a serve setting without a 10-minute soak and
better airflow. Not pursued further now: the VRAM line is worth 2.4x-11x past 16k, clocks 5%."

## Corrections later

- P009 soaked +150 / 215 W / fan 100% on the Turing build: stable and exact but 86 C. P009b with desk fans: 74 C,
  +3.5-4.1%, but 1 of 26 outputs diverged (unresolved). Stock stayed the baseline.
- P038 (outside this range) showed that on the planar-PT engine memory clock is a larger lever (+7.7% at +1000).

## What it changed

Nothing in the serve. The slot 4 profile (+150, 215 W) became the P009 soak candidate.

## Toolbox

- Method: staged Afterburner profiles with a blank VF curve so a flat core offset applies; check that the offset moved
  the median core clock by at least half the step, or void the arm.
- Finding (engine and card specific): on the SOA_ISUM engine decode scaled at about half of the core clock gain. A
  stock 2060 SUPER is power-capped at 175 W during decode (throttle reason 0x4 seen in P009).
- Transfers: on any small card in a closed case, heat sets the ceiling for clock offsets; measure with airflow
  recorded, and alternate arms.
