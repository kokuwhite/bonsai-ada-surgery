## Question

On the current engine (0053 tier-graphs, 0054, sparse code present but off), does q3_0 keep P114's line gain and its
decode gain at 128k / 192k, and its quality at 160k?

## Why

P114 ran on 6a4851286 (no 0053, no sparse). 0053 changes decode past the line, so the q3_0 gain must be measured again
on the engine that would ship it.
