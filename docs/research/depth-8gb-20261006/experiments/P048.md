## Question

Does the engine's existing ADD+RMS_NORM+MUL fusion, un-gated for all GPUs, speed up decode on Turing with identical
output?

## Why

"Census (P41b): 128 residual adds (k_bin_bcast) launch alone per decode step. The engine has an ADD+RMS_NORM+MUL fusion
(ggml_cuda_op_add_rms_norm_fused, no arch-specific code), but it is gated to cc == DGX_SPARK."

## Plan and gate (as frozen before the result)

"Plain model, q4_0, -c 8192 all VRAM, raw server; off/on x3 at 4k/16k; greedy 300-token texts saved per arm (3
prompts) and compared; census with the switch on. Gates: on beats off by more than the off-vs-off spread at 4k; texts
identical (else KL on wikitext before any use); census shows ~128 fewer kernels."

## Method

Not run (jobrun5.sh: off/on x3 with quick_tps at 4k/16k, three 300-token greedy texts per arm, census with the switch
on).

## Results

None.

## Corrections later

- P55b (2026-10-09) showed that the first census graph is the warm-up graph. In the plain decode graph there are 48
  add kernels, not 128; the 128 count is right for the 2-column verify graph of the drafting mode (P55b census_mtp).
- P56 (2026-10-08) measured engine 8a6d2ea12 (with patch 0040) on 8 GB: decode +1-2 %, within round spread.
- 2026-10-09 decision note: "The norm->FWHT fusion target shrank after patch 0040 (48 of 129 norms are already
  ADD+RMS_NORM+MUL)."

## What it changed

The fusion went into the bonsai patch stack as patch 0040 (PR #8) instead of a home-made un-gate.

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Before you write a fusion, search upstream for one (here PrismML #209 existed).
- Transfers: a fusion gated to one device (cc == DGX_SPARK) may be valid on all GPUs; test correctness
  (test-backend-ops ADD / RMS_NORM) and output identity before you un-gate it.
