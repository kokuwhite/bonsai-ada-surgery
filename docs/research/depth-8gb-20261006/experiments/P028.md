## Question

Does the RC2 build (release architectures) reproduce the dev-build numbers of both final modes, with no one-token answers?

## Why

P025-P027 set the final modes on bin-p25 (sm_75 only). A release must be verified on its own multi-architecture binaries.

## Plan and gate

No separate plan entry. p28.sh (the frozen script) runs both modes once at 4k/16k/32k/60k and counts one-token answers.
The result compares with the dev builds ("within 1%") and the floor.

## Method

p28.sh waits for "build-rc2 exit", refuses if the build failed, then drafting (06:46:21) and long (06:53:16) through
run_arm.sh.
