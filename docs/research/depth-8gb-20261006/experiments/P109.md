## Question

Do the sparse arms lose speed on 8 GB because they over-commit VRAM? With 200 MiB more margin, does sparse end
with free VRAM > 0, and do 245k decode and the 160k follow-up prefill recover?

## Why

Found by the P90-P108 record writer: every sparse arm in P102-P106 ends with nvidia-smi memory.free = 4194274-4194296
MiB (wrapped: used > total by 8-30 MiB = WDDM over-commit); every dense arm ends with 107-124 MiB free. P107 then
showed the wrap at the first sparse decode step and the ternary mat-vecs 2.5-24x slower after it.

## Plan and gate

Notebook: "P109: P106 env + BONSAI_VRAM_MARGIN=425 (default 225 with drafting): sparse 160k+245k, dense 245k. Gate
(frozen in the script header): cause confirmed if sparse ends with free > 0 AND (245k sparse > same-margin dense OR
160k follow-up prefill >= 100 tok/s). If confirmed, the launcher must count the sparse buffers when it sizes the
line." Script header: "Gate (declared here, before the run): over-commit is the cause if sparse with the margin ends
with free > 0 AND (245k sparse decode > the same-margin dense 245k OR the 160k 512-token follow-up prefill recovers
to >= 100 tok/s, P106 dense 107, sparse 85)."

## Method

run_arm.sh: sparse_m425 with quick_tps 160000 then 245000 on one server; then dense_m425 at 245000. Summary: one-token
count, depth lines, mean decode, min free, and the launcher's kv tier lines.

## Verdict

VOID (no data) and SUPERSEDED. The 4070's answer on #21 (2026-10-13 16:02Z) measured what 028f606fc allocates only
with GGML_CUDA_FA_SPARSE, at the first sparse op (the first decode step): (1) page-bound tables, cudaMalloc, f32 max +
min per page / head / dim: 227 MiB at 160k, 393 MiB at 245k (2 KiB per host cell); (2) the first fill, the whole host
tail of K as f16, a pool temporary kept by the pool's high-water mark: 219 / 385 MiB; other < 35 MiB. That is ~446 MiB
at 160k and up to ~780 MiB at 245k above the dense arm. A +200 MiB margin cannot cover it, so P109 could not settle
the question at either depth.

## Corrections later

P111 (fixed build 8d5b862fc path, margin 475) answered the question: over-commit was the cause (sparse min free 106
MiB, mat-vecs back at dense times, follow-up prefill equal to dense). P109b (old build, margin 1025) is queued to show
the same on 028f606fc.

## What it changed

Nothing in the engine or the launcher. Its plan led to the #21 question that produced the allocation answer and the
8d5b862fc fix (events E21).

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Method: before you size a margin test, ask (or measure) how big the suspected allocation is at each depth. A
  margin below that size cannot confirm or reject the cause.
- Method: a "done" line in a queue log is not a result. Mark an interrupted job with a NOTE line and keep its partial
  receipt under a separate name (here .INTERRUPTED and .STOPPED-1013).
